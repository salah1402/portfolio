## 5. Services Offered

Every service explicitly mentioned across the site:

### Comprehensive Spatial Solutions:
1. **Residential Interiors**: Complete turnkey home design, living suites, foyers, and bespoke spatial solutions.
2. **Modular Kitchen Planning & Execution**: Custom island layouts, quartz waterfall surfaces, matte cabinetry, and ergonomic work triangles.
3. **Master Bedroom & Wardrobe Systems**: Sliding wardrobes, acoustic panelling, vanity bays, and smoked glass walk-in dressing suites.
4. **Living, Dining & Joinery Architecture**: Fluted millwork, ambient lighting, illuminated portal frames, architectural display cabinetry, and decorative metal/glass partitions.
5. **Business & Retail Interiors**: Custom commercial interiors for retail outlets, showrooms, and consumer-facing retail spaces.
6. **Office & Corporate Interiors**: Workplace layout planning, executive suites, modular workstations, and collaborative breakout zones.

### Architectural Planning & Technical Documentation:
7. **Floor & Furniture Plans**: Precision spatial layouts mapped to circulation paths, room dimensions, and functional living requirements.
8. **Reflected Ceiling Plans (RCP)**: Architectural ceiling layouts specifying fixture drops, lighting coordinates, and integrated ceiling treatments.
9. **Interior Elevations**: Scaled vertical drawings illustrating wall finishes, cabinetry dimensions, paneling heights, and joinery details.
10. **Kitchen Plans**: Ergonomic cabinetry storage mapping, appliance layout planning, counter surfaces, and workflow organization.
11. **Electrical Plans**: Schematics mapping electrical power outlets, switches, circuit loads, and lighting points coordinated with furniture layouts.

### Maintenance & Specialized Architectural Works:
12. **Glass-Related Services with Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)**: Specialized architectural glass installations and fittings accompanied by dedicated annual maintenance support across Bengaluru.
